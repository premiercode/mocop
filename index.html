Meet our pastors

Called, tested and faithful.

Dr. Sam Fenceroy built a chain of twenty-three stores, then answered God’s call to ministry. Today he serves as Senior Pastor of Mt. Olive and a presbyter with Christ For The Nations’ Fellowship of Ministries and Churches.

Pastor Gloria Fenceroy, Assistant Senior Pastor, gave thirty-eight years to Dallas ISD as a counselor and is a licensed professional counselor. She leads our Women of Word ministry and the beloved Testimonial Tea. Together they have four adult daughters.

Our story

It all started in a library in Plano, Texas.

From those humble beginnings, God expanded the vision. Today, Mt. Olive is planting churches all over the world, buying Bibles, digging wells and helping build sanctuaries so pastors and new believers have a place to worship.

And here at home, we still celebrate every new beginning. Water baptism is a public declaration that a new life has begun. All who have received Jesus are welcome.
